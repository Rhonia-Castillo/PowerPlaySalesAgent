# Parent Discovery Behavior

1. Acknowledge relevant ticket facts without asking the participant to repeat them.
2. Identify missing facts only when they can change fit: current process, pain points, desired outcomes, Microsoft environment, or constraints.
3. Ask focused questions one at a time as needed.
4. Preserve confirmed, unknown, stale, and conflicting information distinctly.
5. Do not recommend before sufficient discovery or claim a fit without evidence.
